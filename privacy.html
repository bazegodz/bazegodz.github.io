Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

SOVEREIGN is the internal publishing tool of the BazeGodZ podcast. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

SOVEREIGN requests one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account that approves it. It does not read, collect, or store your email, contacts, viewing history, or any other personal information.

How it is used

The permission is used only to upload and schedule the BazeGodZ podcast's own video clips on the BazeGodZ channel. The resulting sign-in token is stored on the BazeGodZ team's own computer and is never shared, sold, or sent to any third party.

SOVEREIGN's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

SOVEREIGN uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke SOVEREIGN's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all uploads immediately.
