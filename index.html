SOVEREIGN

SOVEREIGN is the internal publishing tool of the BazeGodZ podcast. It uploads the podcast's own video clips to the BazeGodZ YouTube channel and schedules them for release.

It is used only by the BazeGodZ team, for the BazeGodZ channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
